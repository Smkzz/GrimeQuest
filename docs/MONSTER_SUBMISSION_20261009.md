## Positioning

**GrimeQuest — Your mess. Their problem.**

A tiny monster battle that ends with one real chore done. The pitch is not an AI cleaning inspector or another task manager. It is a playful reason to start and finish one small task.

## Ready-to-paste writeup (359 characters)

Your mess. Their problem. GrimeQuest turns a real cleaning chore into a tiny monster battle: snap the dirt, clean it, and evict a fictional grime creature. Reveal your before/after, earn self-reported XP and collect six original monsters. No accounts, uploads, cleaner menus or AI setup. Try the illustrated demo in 15 seconds, then defeat a real little mess.

## Strongest 45–60-second video

**0–6 seconds:** Show one real, ordinary mess and say, “This little freeloader has been living here rent-free.” Cut to Smudgie in GrimeQuest. Keep the intro focused on the real chore, not a feature list.

**6–16 seconds:** Tap Find some grime and take the actual before photo. Show the fictional monster on it. “No account. No product menus. Just one spot.”

**16–30 seconds:** Film the actual cleaning using independently suitable care and the actual label instructions. Label any time-lapse. Never shorten or misrepresent required product handling or use an uncertain surface to make a better video.

**30–42 seconds:** Take the real after photo and confirm your own result. Show the monster celebration and before/after slider. Say, “The app motivates the chore; it does not claim to inspect hygiene.”

**42–52 seconds:** Show the new creature in My wins and progress toward the next level. “One chore, one little victory. Your photos stay on your phone.”

**52–60 seconds:** Show the playable link and public repository. “Your mess. Their problem.”

## Use the illustrated demo honestly

The built-in demo is ideal for visitors who are not near a cleaning task. It uses original illustrated scenes and awards no XP or collection entries. A recording or screenshot of it must remain labeled illustrated/demo. Do not present that scene as a real household before/after or as evidence that someone cleaned.

The supplied rendered screenshots and illustrated video are presentation assets, not physical-device acceptance. For the main submission card, use the home-screen screenshot: it shows the real interface, primary action and a recognizable character without inventing a cleaning result.
