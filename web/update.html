Will this delete my data or offline copy?

No inventory, quest journal, account details or photos are deleted from local storage. GrimeQuest keeps the old offline app until the complete replacement has been downloaded and activated. If the server temporarily responds “Service Unavailable,” the update pauses safely rather than deleting the working cache. Unfinished in-memory photos will be lost only when you leave the active quest page.
