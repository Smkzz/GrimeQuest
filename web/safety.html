SAFETY BOUNDARIES

The game never outranks the label.

Prototype safety boundary · updated 2026-10-08

Follow the real product and surface instructions

GrimeQuest is a narrow prototype, not a chemical-safety certification. Use only an exact product that you own, read its current directions and warnings, and confirm the surface manufacturer's care instructions before use. When those instructions conflict with the app, stop and follow the manufacturer.

Never mix or layer cleaners

The game does not reward combining products, switching products mid-task or using extra chemical to chase a score. If another cleaner may already be present, stop the quest and follow the product instructions for safe handling or rinsing.

All known surfaces can be guided camera quests

Glass, glazed ceramic, stainless steel, cool unpowered glass-ceramic hobs, natural stone, wood and another material the user identifies from its real care instructions may be tracked in the self-reported guided mode, including visible mineral deposits. This does not mean GrimeQuest has approved a cleaner, descaler, tool or procedure for them. The player must independently verify the exact product/tool and surface care directions before cleaning. No AI safety assertion, guaranteed cleaning outcome or chemical advice is provided.

Situations that still require a stop

  • Unknown material or unknown residue: identify the target before selecting a method
  • Damaged, unusually delicate or unsafe targets
  • Hot or powered appliances and electrical hazards
  • Mould, body fluids, unknown chemicals or other significant hazards
  • Bleach, drain cleaners, strong acids, solvents or other high-risk products outside the reviewed catalog

The reviewed-product recommendation catalog remains narrow. A surface being playable in guided mode never enables or expands a catalog cleaning-product match; all exact label restrictions still apply.

What the camera cannot prove

A photo may show visible change. It cannot establish disinfection, microbiological cleanliness, hidden residues, the exact material composition of a surface, or who performed the cleaning. GrimeQuest therefore uses clear, partial and unverifiable visual outcomes rather than “percent clean.”

If something goes wrong

Stop using the product and follow its label. For exposure, poisoning, breathing difficulty, burns or another urgent health concern, use the appropriate local poison-control or emergency service rather than this app.
