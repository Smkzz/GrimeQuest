PRIVACY NOTICE

Photos are for the quest, not a profile.

Prototype privacy notice · updated 2026-10-09

Your photos stay on your device

The public monster game processes before and after photos inside your browser. It temporarily holds them in memory for the current quest and does not upload them to our servers or an AI provider. They are not saved in your journal or browser local storage. Closing or reloading during a quest loses its unfinished photos. The app may display a leave-page warning, but browser support varies.

What your device remembers

Your XP and recent quest history are stored in browser local storage. Your creature collection and level are calculated from those self-reported wins. No algorithm verifies physical cleaning or hygiene. Up to 200 history entries are retained; clearing browser storage can erase this progress. Old local inventory records and an interrupted-task warning may remain from earlier versions until you explicitly delete local data. Any old private-test access code remains in session storage until you delete local data or close that session.

The demo is only an illustration

The quick demo uses original fictional artwork included in the app. It does not open the camera, record a cleaning win, grant XP, or add creatures to your collection. It is not evidence of a real-world task.

Sharing is optional and contains no photos

Nothing is shared automatically. After a real photo quest, the Share this win button opens your device's sharing interface with a short self-reported win message and the GrimeQuest link. Your before and after pictures are not attached. Where native sharing is unavailable, the app can copy the same text to your clipboard. Any service you choose to share to processes that text under its own terms.

No account, analytics or runtime AI

The casual game has no user account, advertising SDK, behavioral analytics, location collection, contacts access, microphone access or payment integration. Camera permission is requested only after an action to open it. Earlier product-search and optional AI APIs remain in the source for compatibility, but they are not used by the public monster game.

Hosting

Our host serves the app and its static assets. Hosting infrastructure may retain ordinary request and operational metadata under its own policies. The casual game does not send cleaning-photo bytes to that host, and does not create a server-side personal profile or photo library.

Delete local data

Open About and settings (•••), choose Delete local progress, then confirm. This removes local GrimeQuest progress, history, legacy inventory, the interrupted-task warning and old private-test access code. It does not remove anything you previously chose to share to another service.

Keep private details out of the frame

Avoid including people, addresses, mail, screens, documents or reflections in cleaning photos. Point the camera at one small task. The app does not automatically redact visible personal information.

Operator details

The operator still needs to finalize its data-controller identity, privacy contact and applicable handling details before a general public service launch. This prototype notice does not claim a completed legal-compliance review. Live AI analysis is not part of this game.
