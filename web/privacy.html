PRIVACY NOTICE

Photos are for the quest, not a profile.

Prototype privacy notice · updated 2026-10-06

What stays on your device

GrimeQuest stores your product inventory, quest journal and interrupted-task warning in browser local storage. The private live-mode access code is kept in session storage for the current browser tab. Photos are held in page memory for the current quest and are not written into the journal or local storage.

When a photo leaves your device

Practice examples and Guided Camera Quests never upload photos. In Guided Camera Quests, your before and after pictures are compared by you, not by AI; results and XP are self-reported. For automatic label text reading, you separately consent to sending the two resized label photos to the GrimeQuest server for in-memory Finnish/English OCR (no external AI provider or photo file). In optional AI Beta, a photo is sent only after you explicitly approve that analysis. The browser re-encodes the image before upload and the server strips image metadata again. Visible information inside the photo is not automatically redacted.

The GrimeQuest server forwards normalized images for consented AI Beta to the operator-selected provider. When OpenRouter is used, routing requests require Zero Data Retention and disable provider data collection, but the service and upstream provider still process your photos and their privacy terms apply. The app shows the provider destination when live mode is configured.

Server-side handling

The application does not intentionally persist uploaded photos or extracted product-label text. Signed workflow tokens contain bounded task metadata, not image bytes; comparison-result deduplication is held only in process memory and expires or disappears on restart. Hosting infrastructure may retain ordinary request and operational metadata according to its own policies.

No account or analytics in this release

This release has no user accounts, advertising SDK, behavioral analytics, location collection, contacts access, microphone access or payment integration. Camera permission is requested only when you choose to open the camera.

Delete your local data

Use Settings → Delete my local data to remove the local inventory, journal, access code and interrupted-task warning from that browser. Because the server does not maintain a user account or photo library, there is no server-side personal profile to delete in this release.

Good photo hygiene

Avoid including people, addresses, mail, screens, documents, reflections or other private details in cleaning photos. Point the camera at one small cleaning target.

Before public launch

The operator must add the final data-controller identity, privacy contact, legal basis, retention details and the selected vision provider's processing terms before presenting live AI analysis as a generally available service.
