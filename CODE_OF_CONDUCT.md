# Code of conduct

GrimeQuest welcomes constructive contributions from people of different
backgrounds and experience levels. The aim is a friendly, accessible
open-source project that makes everyday chores more approachable.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, kind and specific in technical feedback.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for the impact of your words.
- Share reproduction steps without exposing sensitive photos, credentials
  or another person's identifying information.
- Credit original authors and honor third-party licenses.
- Help newcomers understand the shipped game and its documented limitations.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, doxxing, unwanted disclosure
of personal data, impersonation and sustained personal attacks are not
welcome. Do not pressure contributors to perform unsafe cleaning or publish
private information.

## Scope and moderation

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other channels
moderated by the GrimeQuest maintainers. Maintainers may request changes,
remove inappropriate contributions where supported, lock threads or block
accounts for repeated or serious abuse.

For nonsensitive concerns, open a short GitHub issue without private
details. For sensitive conduct concerns, request a private maintainer contact
route without describing the incident publicly. Security vulnerabilities
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public bug report.

This is an independent project policy; it does not claim a dedicated
external moderation organization or an unverified private email address.
